Sonef ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the Sonef customer app).

We collect information that you provide directly to us or that is generated when you use the app, including:

We use the information we collect to:
